Shipping Policy

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation with tracking details so you can monitor the delivery status.
Shipping Locations
Delsi’s Chews currently ships across India. We work with trusted courier partners to ensure your pet treats reach you safely and quickly.
Shipping Time
Estimated delivery times depend on your location.
Metro Cities: 2–4 business days
Other Cities and Towns: 3–6 business days
Remote Areas: 5–8 business days
Please note that delivery times may vary due to courier delays, weather conditions, or unforeseen logistical issues.
Shipping Charges
Shipping charges may vary depending on the order value, delivery location, and ongoing promotions.
In many cases, we offer free shipping on eligible orders. Any applicable shipping charges will be displayed during checkout before payment.
Packaging
All Delsi’s Chews products are carefully packed in secure, hygienic packaging to maintain freshness and prevent damage during transit.
We use sealed pouches and protective packaging materials to ensure the product reaches you in perfect condition.
Order Tracking
Once your order is shipped, tracking details will be shared via email or SMS. You can use this information to track your shipment through the courier partner’s website.
Delivery Issues

If your order is marked as delivered but you have not received it, please inform our support team within 48 hours so we can investigate with the courier partner.

Return &amp; Refund Policy
At Delsi’s Chews, we are committed to providing high-quality natural treats for your pets. Customer satisfaction and pet safety are our top priorities. If you experience any issues with your order, please review our return and refund policy below.
Eligibility for Returns
Due to the nature of pet food and hygiene regulations, returns are only accepted under the following conditions:
• The product received is damaged, defective, or spoiled.
• You received the wrong product in your order.
• The product packaging was tampered with during delivery.
To be eligible for a return, the request must be made within 
48 hours of receiving the order
.
Non-Returnable Items
For safety and hygiene reasons, we do not accept returns in the following cases:
• Opened or partially consumed products
• Change of mind after delivery
• Products stored improperly after delivery
• Products returned after the allowed return period
Proof Required for Return
To process a return or refund request, customers must provide:
• Order number
• Clear photos of the product received
• Photos of damaged packaging (if applicable)
These details help us review and resolve your request quickly.
Refund Process
Once your return request is reviewed and approved:
• Refunds will be processed within 
5–7 business days
.
• Refunds will be issued to the 
original payment method
 used during purchase.
• In some cases, we may provide a 
replacement product instead of a refund
.
Delivery Issues
If your order was not delivered or shows delivery issues, please contact our support team within 
3 days of the expected delivery date
 so we can assist you.
Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled only 
before they are shipped
